# Establishing System Operating Limits for the Operations Horizon | Project Pillar: Technical Specs
**Date:** May 2026
**Status:** Technical Documentation

## 🎯 Executive Summary
This document is CAISO's Operating Procedure No. 3100, detailing the methodology, responsibilities, and guidelines for establishing System Operating Limits (SOLs) and Interconnection Reliability Operating Limits (IROLs) for the CAISO Controlled Grid. It implements the NERC FAC-011 and FAC-014 standards across the seasonal, outage planning, next-day, same-day, and real-time horizons.

## 🔑 Key Arguments & Findings
* **Finding 1:** Reliability Coordinators must maintain a documented methodology for establishing SOLs and IROLs to ensure grid stability under pre-contingency conditions and credible single/multiple contingency events.
* **Finding 2:** CAISO System Operators are vested with full authority to take immediate real-time corrective actions, including firm load shedding, to alleviate limit exceedances and maintain reliability.

## 🛠️ Methodological Notes / Technical Specifications
* **Regulatory Compliance:** NERC Standards FAC-011-4 and FAC-014-3, WECC reliability criteria.
* **Operations Horizon:** Rolling 12-month period.
* **Analysis Categories:** Steady-state post-contingency limits, post-transient voltage stability, transient stability limits, and nuclear power interface requirements.
* **Systems Involved:** CAISO Market Application, Customer Market Results Interface (CMRI).

## 💡 Personal Insights & Open Questions
* **Potential Application:** The CAISO SOL definitions provide real-world context for grid constraints that motivate EGoT's active power flow reservation limits. EGoT services should ideally map local feeder thresholds to equivalent system operating limits.
* **Gap/Next Step:** The document focuses entirely on transmission-level (bulk power system) operating limits. Bridging the gap between transmission SOLs and distribution-level dynamic operating limits for DERs remains an open area of research.
