# The Plug-and-Play Electricity Era: Interoperability to Integrate Anything, Anywhere, Anytime | Project Pillar: Literature Review
**Date:** May 2026
**Status:** Peer-Reviewed Literature

## 🎯 Executive Summary
This paper examines the role of interoperability in the modern smart grid, introducing the concept of a "plug-and-play" electricity era. It discusses how context-setting frameworks, maturity models, and standard-aligned cybersecurity practices allow diverse cyber-physical systems to exchange and utilize data efficiently.

## 🔑 Key Arguments & Findings
* **Finding 1:** Integrating modern grid devices requires not just physical wiring, but the connection of computer-based automation, sensing, and secure communications, demanding architectural modularity and composability.
* **Finding 2:** Interoperability reduces integration costs, improves overall grid efficiency, and facilitates cybersecurity by replacing proprietary, ad-hoc connections with standardized, testable interfaces.

## 🛠️ Methodological Notes / Technical Specifications
* **Interoperability Standard:** ISO/IEC/IEEE Standard 24765.
* **Key Frameworks:** Interoperability Maturity Model (IMM), GridWise Interoperability Context-Setting Framework, and NIST Smart Grid Interoperability Roadmap.
* **Case Study:** Smart EV Charging integration detailing authentication, device registry, and dynamic price-rate configuration.

## 💡 Personal Insights & Open Questions
* **Potential Application:** Validating EGoT's interoperability goals. Adherence to standards like IEEE 2030.5 and CSIP is what transforms EGoT from a custom sandbox into a true "plug-and-play" testing environment for utilities and manufacturers.
* **Gap/Next Step:** While maturity models provide a high-level assessment of integration, they do not offer concrete, automated software unit and integration test scripts to verify compliance at the API endpoint level.

## 📌 Critical Quotes
> "ISO/IEC/IEEE standard 24765 defines interoperability as 'the ability of two or more systems or components to exchange information and to use the information that has been exchanged.'"
